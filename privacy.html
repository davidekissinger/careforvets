Privacy

Website privacy notice

This website is intended for general information and business inquiries. It is not a patient portal or clinical communication system.

Information you provide

If you submit the contact form, CareForVets may receive your name, email address, phone number, organization, role and message. Please do not submit protected health information or medical records through this website.

How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential partnerships, improve the site and maintain reasonable security and operational records.

Clinical information

This website is not configured as a clinical record system. If CareForVets later offers clinical services, separate privacy notices, secure systems and applicable HIPAA processes will govern protected health information.

Third-party services

The site may be hosted or processed using third-party infrastructure. Those services may process technical information necessary to provide the website. Production infrastructure may change as the CareForVets model develops.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be sent to info@careforvets.com.
