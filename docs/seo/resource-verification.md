# Public resource verification

Source review date: **2026-11-06**. Dataset: `src/content/resources.mjs`.

## Scope and meaning of verification

All 18 records were checked against the official organization or government pages linked below. The review covered the resource's purpose, intended audience, public contact route, and stated access limits. The `checked` field records this review date. It does not mean an office answered a call, an application was accepted, an appointment is available, or CareForVets has a relationship with the organization. No forms were submitted and no services were booked.

The records provide navigation and education. They do not diagnose, prescribe, determine benefit eligibility, or endorse a provider's clinical quality. Names, phone numbers, schedules, and restrictions come from the official sources. No SparkToro traffic or affinity estimate was used to establish service availability. Optional `cost` fields are omitted; the few references to fees or free assistance in the visible copy are specifically supported by the sources.

Categories are limited to `care-at-home`, `caregivers`, `disability`, `mental-health`, `benefits`, and `careers`. A resource may belong to more than one category. The rendered card text (name, summary, audience, geography, and access) is intended to remain between 50 and 95 words.

## Geographic and operational limits

- The directory contains national programs and online information. It makes no claim that an in-person service is available in Toledo, elsewhere in Ohio, or Michigan. A national office network is not proof of an available local appointment.
- VA home care and respite depend on location, assessed needs, eligibility, and agency availability. The directory does not represent CareForVets as a VA-contracted care provider or as the referral authority.
- The Veterans Crisis Line call/text instructions are U.S. access routes. Overseas users should follow the official site's regional instructions.
- PVA's career program is broader than an assumption that only veterans with paralysis can use it. WWP's current registration wording must be checked when updating the record; the program name alone should not be used to infer injury criteria.
- Hidden Heroes/EDF Connect is a caregiver community with verification requirements. Its inclusion is not permission to advertise in the community, access member information, or claim a partnership.
- The guessed BIAA deep URL `/brain-injury/about-brain-injury/nbiic` could not be retrieved. The published record uses the verified home page, which displays the information center contact and caregiver links.
- RallyPoint, private benefits firms, and unconfirmed local resources are excluded. Cohen Veterans Network and Veterans Inc. are not included in this launch set because the planned local-service implications require separate geographic verification.

## Maintenance and implementation notes

Reopen the official sources before a public release if the source-review date has become stale, and check access information whenever a broken link or changed program is reported. Update `checked` only after reviewing the actual official content; a successful HTTP status is not sufficient. Keep secondary verification links in this file when the card has only one `sourceUrl` field.

Preserve each card's audience, geography, access limit, source link, and date when rendering it. Do not relabel resources as CareForVets partners, approved providers, guaranteed benefits, or available placements. Leave crisis-resource interactions untracked by marketing systems, and do not send selected health categories, resource identifiers, referral destinations, or inferred conditions to third-party analytics. No forms or service requests are implemented by this dataset.
